# Acenara identity and original artwork

The current mark is a sculptural ribbon A with a coral sphere. The outlined Manrope wordmark uses mist blue for Acen and coral for ara; light and dark variants retain the same geometry. The mark, favicon, Apple icon, application icon, and social artwork are generated by `scripts/build-brand.mjs`. The earlier exploration sheet is retained as design history, not the selected final mark.

The hero sculpture is an original conceptual brand composition. It is not a photograph of premises or a physical company asset. Its original source remains `public/images/brand-sculpture-original.png`, and its optimized delivery asset is `public/images/brand-sculpture.webp`.

## Redesign asset collection

Eight additional original images were created with the built-in image generation tool:

- Technical compositions: AI connections, a multi-device product, cloud infrastructure, and layered security.
- Representative collaboration scenes: a diverse product team, a software architecture discussion, a strategy consultation, and a UI/UX design review.

The human subjects and environments are fictional and representative. Visible captions identify these images as generated. They are not Acenara employee, client, or office photographs. No third-party stock photography or client screenshots were used. Product dashboards, calendar interactions, and technical illustrations are original React, CSS, and SVG compositions with illustrative data.

The source PNGs are in `design/source-images/`. Exact prompts, generation metadata, and source-to-output mapping are recorded in `design/asset-prompts.json`. Run `npm run images:build` to produce 1440px optimized WebP assets under `public/images/`. The source directory is excluded from the public static export by its location.

Human imagery is used across Home, About, Services and service details, Solutions, How We Work, and Contact, with subtle blue shadows, coral context accents, responsive crops, and restrained hover transitions.

## Generation prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide landscape hero artwork for Acenara, a premium software and digital technology studio. Create a sophisticated original editorial 3D still life of two tall sculptural interlocking architectural ribbons forming an abstract open letter A, one matte mist blue #2F5061 and one warm ivory #F7F5F2 with a single small coral pink #E57F84 spherical accent. A stepped plinth, sculptural precise folds, elegant chamfered edges, fine mineral texture, dramatic but soft side lighting and long quiet shadows. Background a continuous warm light ivory cyclorama, no real office, no people, no screens, no text, no logos, no watermarks. Art directed premium design studio campaign, restrained contemporary museum sculpture, photographed orthographically with slight elevated perspective, subject centered-right and full sculpture uncropped with generous negative space. Landscape 3:2 composition. This is a conceptual brand sculpture, never a company office photo. Very high finish, tactile material realism, refined engineering.
